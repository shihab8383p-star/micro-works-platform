# 🎯 REPLIT - COMPLETE GUIDE (2 MINUTES)

## Step 1: Go to Replit (30 seconds)
- Visit: https://replit.com
- Click "Sign up"
- Choose "Continue with GitHub" or "Continue with Google"

## Step 2: Import Your Project (1 minute)
- Click "Create Repl" (big blue button)
- Click "Import from GitHub"
- Paste: `https://github.com/shihab8383p-star/micro-works-platform`
- Click "Import from GitHub" button

## Step 3: Wait for Setup (30 seconds)
- Replit automatically:
  - Detects Node.js
  - Installs all packages (npm install)
  - Sets up environment

## Step 4: Add Environment Variables (30 seconds)
- Click "Tools" (left sidebar) → "Secrets"
- Add these secrets (click "+ New Secret" for each):

| Key | Value |
|-----|-------|
| `MONGODB_URI` | `mongodb://localhost:27017/microworks` |
| `JWT_SECRET` | `your-secret-key-12345` |
| `PORT` | `5000` |
| `MIN_WITHDRAWAL` | `500` |

## Step 5: Click "Run" (10 seconds)
- Click the big green "Run" button at top
- Wait 10-20 seconds
- **YOUR WEBSITE IS LIVE!**

## Step 6: Get Your URL
- Look at top - you'll see URL like: `https://micro-works-platform.replit.app`
- Visit: `https://your-url.replit.app/setup` to initialize database
- **DONE! WEBSITE WORKING!**
